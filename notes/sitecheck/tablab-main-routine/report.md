## Fixes I can make

- **TL-01 — Desktop Boardroom descriptions overlap.** At 1440×900 the cards are 159px high while descriptions reach 186–210px. Rebalance the card columns/height and preserve the approved background. Evidence: chromium/1440x900-boardroom.png; focus/report.json desktop overlap measurement.
- **TL-02 — Narrow Boardroom names are clipped.** At 320×568 the label boxes have 93px width but NOTICEBOARD and SPRINGBOARD need 126–127px; the cards clip their contents. Let names wrap or adjust the narrow card composition. Evidence: focus/narrow-boardroom.png; final/report.json narrow label measurements.
- **TL-03 — Short-landscape Boardroom cannot be scrolled normally.** At 780×300 content extends to 375px, but html overflow:hidden prevents wheel scrolling over the cards. Bottom cards are cut off. Keyboard focus can move the viewport, which does not solve touch/pointer reachability. Allow page scrolling when its content exceeds the viewport. Evidence: final/short-boardroom-wheel-content.png and scroll container measurements. This conflicts with keeping this composition strictly non-scrollable at this height.
- **TL-04 — Board gallery contrast needs correction.** Noticeboard kicker 2.43:1; Skateboard kicker 2.53:1 and body copy 3.83:1; Springboard active tab 4.15:1. These normal-size text elements require 4.5:1. Adjust existing text/panel colours while keeping their palettes. Evidence: chromium/report.json axe scans and gallery captures. Discard the two automated Noticeboard gallery-heading warnings: the scanner inferred a #111 background, but the inspected capture has a light background, sampled RGB(232,223,195).
- **TL-05 — Menu and landing keyboard focus need managing.** With the menu open, Tab passes Contact and reaches the underlying Make me a site and Let me choose controls. Changing to the enquiry panel leaves focus on body until another Tab. Keep focus within the open menu including its close control, restore it on close, and place focus on the newly shown landing panel/action. Evidence: focus/report.json complete 15-step focus trace and enquiry keyboard transition.
- **TL-06 — Screen-reader structure needs small corrections.** The landing h1 is visually display:none and absent from the accessibility tree. Brand links sit outside landmarks on About, Care, Why Website and Boardroom. Expose a meaningful heading and include brand navigation within header/navigation landmarks without changing the visual design. Evidence: chromium/report.json axe scans; source index.html and inner-page headers.
- **TL-07 — Enquiry email text uses the wrong voice.** The customer’s outgoing draft starts “Hi, I'm Rob” and describes Tab Lab’s response in Rob’s voice. Replace this with a customer enquiry addressed to Rob and useful prompts. Preserve tablab@rkdb.nz, subject and mailto behaviour; no real message sent. Evidence: public/script.js setStartEmailLinks and checked enquiry href.
- **TL-08 — Gallery preview images are oversized.** One normal Noticeboard gallery load transfers about 6.85MB across its three preview images (2.43, 2.20 and 2.22MB), despite small display sizes. Produce appropriately sized compressed derivatives of the same images; do not generate new artwork. Evidence: final/report.json normal load resource sample. Local timings are not an internet speed benchmark.
