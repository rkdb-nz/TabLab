Elinor Hart

Elinor Hart makes small runs of everyday ceramics in a converted apple store. Slowly, and with intention.

“I trained in St Ives and spent three years apprenticing in Mashiko, Japan, before returning to establish Ash & Oak. My work explores the quiet relationship between function and ritual — how a daily object can anchor a moment.”

When she’s not at the wheel, Elinor tends the dye garden, mixes glazes from wood ash collected in the valley, and teaches small groups in the workshop.

A working studio

A converted apple store, with whitewashed walls, reclaimed oak benches, and north-facing windows. In winter, the kiln warms the space. In summer, the doors stay open to the garden.

350+Pieces fired each month
12Natural ash glazes
100%Reclaimed clay

Locally dug stoneware and porcelain. Every scrap reclaimed. Clay and glazes mixed on site; the wheel and kiln powered by renewable energy.

Our approach draws on wabi-sabi: beauty in imperfection, transience, and the trace of the hand.
